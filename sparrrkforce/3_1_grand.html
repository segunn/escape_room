Grand Day Signs

The ship is very important to the pirate's day-to-day, it shapes his every move. From the crow's nest at the top of the mast, the lookout can see what other ships are signalling. Pirates are notoriously rebellious, so they don't always use their signA1s in the prescribed manner.
